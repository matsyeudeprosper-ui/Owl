## What the rule actually does
Shocks fire about 7 times a day in every year. 89–91% of them "flush"
(a bid low under the shock-window low) and 91–94% of those "reclaim" (an
M1 close back above it) inside the 60-minute window — median 0 minutes
to the flush and 1 minute from flush to reclaim, 52–53% of reclaims in
the breach bar itself. The shock window's low is usually its last bar's
low, so the next bar takes it out and closes above it almost by
construction. The confirmation is therefore nearly automatic: it
converts 83% of shocks into trades, entered a median 8 minutes after the
shock at a median R of 76–107 points.

## Answers
1. **Does flush + reclaim improve on the immediate buy?** Marginally (+0.6 points pooled, 3 of 5 years by 2–7 points), from a −22 base to a −21 base. It does not create a positive trade anywhere.
2. **Present across 2022–2026?** The small improvement is inconsistent (2023 and 2025 worse); the negative expectancy is consistent.
3. **Survives 2021?** Not tested: the rule was dead on consumed data, so the blind era stays sealed.
4. **Generic sweep/reclaim vs shock?** Generic reclaims without a shock do better in every year (pooled −8.8 vs −21.5). The initial large shock makes the reclaim worse, not better; the shock is a continuation signal at the 30-minute horizon.
5. **Does no-reclaim identify continuation?** The no-reclaim minority (~9% of flushes) sits ~300 points below the shock low at the deadline; afterwards it is mixed (2026 −19/−38, 2024 +1/+28). Too few and too inconsistent to call.
6. **Share of shocks producing a trade?** 83%.
7. **Typical shock → entry time?** 8 minutes (median), because the flush and reclaim happen in the first bars.
8. **Structural R first-passage favourable?** No: adverse-first 53–65% at +1R vs −1R in every year.
9. **Robust to removing the best trades?** Irrelevant in the right direction: it is negative with them and worse without them.
10. **Candidate for E021?** No.

## Verdict: **C — reclaim confirmation does not create a durable edge.**

The E019 path clue (later MFE after an initial dip) does not turn into a
tradable entry: the flush/reclaim sequence is almost always present and
carries no information; large 5-minute drops are followed, on average and
in every year since 2022, by further losses after costs, whether entered
immediately or after the reclaim. 2021 and 2017–2020 remain sealed for a
genuinely different mechanism.

Production unchanged. E017 frozen and blind.
