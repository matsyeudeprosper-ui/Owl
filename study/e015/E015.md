# E015 — BTC response after forced-liquidation bursts (2026-09-12)

Preregistration frozen and committed before results (`PREREG_E015.md`,
commit 996753d; inherits the 2026-08-01 preregistration). Data: OKX
BTC-USDT-SWAP forced liquidations recorded live since 2026-07-31 (74 695
fills), Exness Pro BTCUSD bid M1 with the per-bar spread, overlap
2026-07-31 → 2026-09-11 (42 days). Costs $12 per round trip (spread $7 +
$5 slippage). Scripts `e015.py`, `e015_checks.py`; tables
`results_e015.txt`, `results_e015_checks.txt`; events `events_e015.csv`;
control distribution `control_sims.npy`. V4 untouched, production
unchanged. Light study (M1 + CSV).

## Events

Cascade = 5-min bucket whose liquidated size ≥ the 95th percentile of the
trailing 7-day non-empty buckets (amended from 30 days because the feed
is 43 days old; the 30-day rule is reported too). Consecutive large
buckets are one episode; entry at the first M1 close at or after the
episode ends. 113 episodes (56 forced selling, 57 forced buying), median
28 BTC per episode against a threshold of 15 BTC. Context: open interest
falls 0.23% in the cascade bucket on average; taker flow leans with the
forced side (+0.11 imbalance on forced-selling events).

## Robustness checks (predeclared in commit bcefd11 before they ran)

### (a) Latency — this is the decisive one

| entry delay after the bucket ends | reversal +5 min | +15 min | +30 min |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 min (as above) | **+35** (64%) | +9 | +24 |
| 1 min | **+5** (54%) | −19 | −5 |
| 2 min | −16 (53%) | −31 | −11 |
| 5 min | −25 (50%) | −38 | −16 |
| 10 min | −26 (45%) | −15 | −15 |

The snap-back is complete within the first minute or two after the
cascade bucket closes. The zero-delay entry assumes the cascade's size is
known the instant its 5-minute bucket ends and the order fills at that
bar's close. The live recorder polls the OKX REST endpoint every 60 s
(`LIQ_POLL = 60` in `recorder/derivs_recorder.py`), so a cascade is known
0–60 s after its last fill and its 5-min bucket is complete only at the
bucket boundary; with one minute of detection latency the edge is
+5 points net, with two it is gone. A websocket liquidation feed and a
sub-minute bucket definition would be needed to trade this, and that is a
different, unfrozen experiment.

### (b) Volatility-matched price-only control — the mechanism is real, not just "big move reverts"

| event set (top-5% |5-min move|, trailing 7 days, price only) | n | REV 1 min | REV 5 min | REV 15 min |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| all large price moves | 449 | −5 | −6 | −14 |
| large moves WITHOUT a liquidation cascade | 369 | −7 | −10 | −11 |
| large moves WITH a liquidation cascade | 80 | +2 | +10 | −27 |
| liquidation cascades WITH a large move | 79 | **+35** | **+50** | +31 |
| liquidation cascades WITHOUT a large move | 34 | −15 | +2 | −44 |

Large 5-minute moves on their own do not revert after costs. The
reversal belongs to the moves that were forced — and only to those. The
liquidation feed carries information that price alone does not. (The
asymmetry between rows 3 and 4 is the episode definition: row 3 uses the
price bucket's end, row 4 the cascade's end.)

### (c) Equity (0.02 lot, zero latency)

rev 5 min: +$80, maxDD $20, weekly +1 / +5 / +28 / +4 / +35 / +7. rev
15 min: +$19, maxDD $67, two red weeks. The tradable content is the
first 5 minutes.

## Answers to the pass criteria

- Clears costs: yes at 1 and 5 minutes, at zero latency.
- Clears matched random controls: yes, z +9.3 / +6.8; both halves.
- Beyond a price-only volatility match: yes.
- Harvestable: not at 0.02 lot in 42 days (+$80); the sequence has no
  drawdown to speak of, so size is the lever, but see latency.
- Tradable as specified: **no** — one minute of feed latency removes most
  of it, two minutes all of it.

**Verdict: B.** A real, mechanism-consistent post-liquidation snap-back
exists on Exness BTCUSD (forced flow overshoots, price returns within
1–5 minutes; not present after unforced moves of the same size), it
survives costs and 1 000 matched controls and both halves, but it is
not yet tradable: its life is shorter than the detection latency of the
current 5-minute bucket + REST polling pipeline. Owl structure was not
tested as an execution tool (the prereg allowed that only for a tradable
A). Forward-watch: keep the recorder running (the feed is the asset) and
measure the real detection latency; if a sub-minute feed is built, the
frozen test can be rerun on data collected after 2026-09-12 with a
shorter bucket, registered as a new experiment before it is looked at.

Production unchanged.
