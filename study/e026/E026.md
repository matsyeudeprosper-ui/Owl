## Pass criteria
Beats BASELINE on expectancy in TRAIN and TEST: **yes**. In ≥ 3 of 5
eras: **no** (2/5). ≥ 95th percentile of the random masks: **no** (91st).

## Verdict: **C — not adopted.**

The Asian window really is the weaker half of R0, and the mirror arm
proves it there. It does not generalise: on five independent years the
same filter helps twice and hurts three times, and the ranking of hours
is reshuffled in every era. The R0 gain is a property of those 69 days.

Two honest notes for whatever comes next. First, the filter improves
expectancy while reducing total dollars, so it would only be attractive
combined with larger size, which is a different decision. Second, the
hour table above is exactly the kind of chart that invites picking a
window; the preregistration forbids doing that here, and any hour-based
rule must be registered and then tested on data not used in this study.

Production unchanged.
