# E022 — previous-H1 direction gate on the live BOS configuration (2026-09-14)

Owner's hypothesis, registered before results (`PREREG_E022.md`, f549709):
only take trades in the direction of the previous H1 candle, and, for
buys, only above that candle's close (mirror for sells). Frozen live
configuration otherwise. Script `e022.py`; R0 on ticks
(`results_r0.txt`), consumed eras in M1 pess mode with the era's median
spread (`results_<era>.txt`). Production unchanged.

## R0 (2026-07-01 → 09-07, tick execution, $ at 0.02)

| rule | trades | net | PF | wr | exp/trade | maxDD | TRAIN | TEST |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| baseline (frozen live) | 524 | **+236.8** | 1.28 | 59% | +0.45 | 83 | +102.2 | +134.6 |
| R1 direction only | 391 | +15.6 | 1.02 | 54% | +0.04 | 105 | +72.7 | −57.1 |
| **R2 owner rule (direction + beyond H1 close)** | 387 | **+16.1** | 1.02 | 54% | +0.04 | 107 | +73.5 | **−57.4** |
| P1 opposite direction (placebo) | 251 | +183.2 | 1.66 | 62% | +0.73 | 41 | +32.9 | +150.4 |

- The gate passes 69% of signals and removes 213 trades worth **+$176**;
  in TEST it removes +$142 and turns the strategy negative.
- R1 and R2 are the same rule in practice: the "beyond the H1 close"
  condition removes 3 more trades out of 391. A signal that agrees with
  the previous hour's direction is already on the far side of its close.
- P2 random masks (200, same pass rate, M1 pess): mean +127 ± 54, 95th
  +210; R2 +10 sits at the **2.5th percentile** — worse than removing
  the same share of trades at random.
- The placebo is the interesting number: trading AGAINST the previous
  hour's direction keeps most of the profit with half the trades (PF
  1.66, TEST +150). Same contrarian flavour as E014. It was a control,
  not a hypothesis, and see the eras below.
- V1 tail (09-07 → 09-11) is not in the R0 M1 file (ends 09-08 07:11);
  no trades to report.

In every era the profit factor and the expectancy per trade are
unchanged by the gate (to two decimals); the dollar loss shrinks only
because a third of the trades are removed. The removed trades have the
same negative expectancy as the kept ones. The placebo's R0 advantage
does not reproduce cleanly: better per trade in 2023–2024 and 2026 H1,
worse in 2025, equal in 2022.

## Pass criteria
R2 > baseline in TRAIN and TEST: **no** (below in both). ≥ 3 of 5 eras
better in expectancy: **no** (0 of 5; only fewer trades). R2 > P1: **no**
(P1 better everywhere in dollars). ≥ 95th percentile of random masks:
**no** (2.5th). Removed trades negative in both halves: **no** (+35 and
+142 in R0).

## Verdict: **C — the previous-H1 direction carries no information for this strategy.**

On the discovery data the gate deletes the strategy's best trades (it
sits below 97.5% of random deletions); on 2022–2026 it changes nothing
per trade. The price-beyond-close condition is redundant with the
direction condition. If anything the data lean the other way (the
contrarian placebo), but that is not consistent across years and was not
a registered hypothesis. Not adopted; nothing changed in production.
