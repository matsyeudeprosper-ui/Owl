## Answers
1. **Do large 5-min drops continue lower?** No. Gross of costs the short is within ±7 points of zero in every year (+2.4, −7.4, −5.6, +2.2, −3.4): the drop is fully priced by the time the candle closes.
2. **Short positive after costs?** No, −18 to −32 every year.
3. **Across years?** Consistently negative.
4. **Beats matched random shorts?** No (0th to 74th percentile); 2023 and 2026 are significantly worse than random.
5. **Do upside shocks continue up?** No: BUY after a p95 up-move loses −9 to −23 every year. Large 5-minute moves in either direction are followed by noise plus cost.
6. **Survives removing the biggest winners?** It has nothing to survive; removal makes it worse.
7. **Stronger shocks, stronger continuation?** No pattern; the largest tertile ranges from best to worst by year.
8. **How quickly does favourable movement begin?** It mostly does not: adverse-first in 60–81% of events at ±0.5 ATR.
9. **Frequency?** 7/day, ample.
10. **Justify spending 2021?** No.

## Verdict: **C — short continuation is not a durable edge.**

Together with E019 (immediate BUY) and E020 (BUY after reclaim): after a
large 5-minute BTC drop neither direction has expectancy on Exness
BTCUSD in 2022–2026; both sides lose roughly the round-trip cost. The
5-minute shock, on its own, carries no tradable directional information
at 5–60 minutes. 2021 and 2017–2020 remain sealed for a genuinely
different mechanism.
