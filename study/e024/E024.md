# E024 — no 2h rule, and "a touch of the previous H1 high/low resets the structure" (2026-09-15)

Owner's two ideas, preregistered before results (`PREREG_E024.md`,
17b7e30): (a) run the live config without the 2 h awake rule; (b) at each
touch of the previous H1 candle's high or low, wipe every pending pattern
and trade only a pattern that forms afterwards. Script `e024.py`; R0 on
ticks, consumed eras in M1 "pess" mode. Production unchanged.

## How often the reset fires
Two events per hour at most (first touch of the previous hour's high,
first touch of its low): **22 events per day**, stable across every era
(22.0–22.3/day). The structure engine is therefore wiped roughly once an
hour and rarely has time to build anything. On R0 the engine's signal
count falls from 1352 to 268, **−80%**, and what survives is almost
entirely bootstrap flips: the "first signal only" arm produces **zero
continuations**, while continuations carry +$153.74 of the baseline's
+$236.83.

**The smaller dollar losses are volume, not skill.** The reset arms cut
55–90% of the trades, so the loss shrinks proportionally, but the profit
factor gets *worse*: 0.51–0.83 for the reset arms against 0.74–0.90 for
the baseline, worse in 2022, 2023 and 2024 and never convincingly better.
Reading the dollar column alone would be the classic trap of mistaking
"trades less" for "trades better".

## Pass criteria

+$9.44 / +$8.69 against +$102 / +$135). ≥ 95th percentile of the random
masks: **no** (23.5th). Better per trade across the eras: **no** (PF
lower in most cells).

## Answers
- **Without the 2 h rule?** −38% on R0 (+147 vs +237). The rule is worth
  keeping: it discards 143 trades worth −$80, while E023's touch gate
  discarded 22 trades worth +$68. E007 still stands that 2 h itself is
  not special, but removing the gate entirely is a loss.
- **Touch resets the structure?** It converts the strategy into a
  low-volume flip-only system with no continuations, near-zero
  expectancy on R0 and a worse profit factor everywhere else.
- **Anything worth keeping?** "reset + 2 h gate" has the best R0 profit
  factor (1.39) and a $10 drawdown, but 51 trades in 69 days for +$14.64,
  and its era profit factors are worse than the baseline in three years.
  Sample too thin, no consistency: not a lead.

## Verdict: **C — neither idea is adopted.**

Production unchanged.
