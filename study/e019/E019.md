## Answers
1. **Real rebound after a large 5-min drop?** On the whole sample yes (+15 at 15 m, z +4.4), on the independent half no (−7.6).
2. **Survives costs?** Only in the discovery half.
3. **Repeats in both halves?** No.
4. **Beats matched random?** Whole sample yes; check half no (67th percentile).
5. **Robust without the biggest events?** Without the best 2 yes; without the top 10% no (−20).
6. **Mild vs extreme different?** Mid-size shocks carry the result; largest and mildest are near zero after the best-2 removal. No clean severity ordering, nothing to register.
7. **When does the rebound happen?** Late: MFE median at 32 min, after a further ~1 ATR dip in 57% of events; 1 m and 5 m are negative.
8. **Frequency enough?** Yes, 7 events/day.
9. **Realistically executable?** The entry rule is executable (30 s after a 5-min close), but the path (adverse first, slow MFE) means a stop tight enough to be safe cuts the rebound off; there is nothing stable to execute.
10. **One mechanism for E020?** Not from this data. The only remaining live lead is E017 (forward, blind). If the downside-shock idea is revisited it must be on data after 2026-09-11 13:39 UTC, registered first.
