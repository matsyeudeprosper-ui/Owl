# E023 — previous-H1 high/low touch as the eligibility gate (2026-09-15)

Owner's idea: replace the "at least one flip in 2 h" weather rule with
"price must touch the previous H1 candle's high or low before a BOS entry
is considered". Preregistered before results (`PREREG_E023.md`, a3aa3ee).
Script `e023.py`; R0 on ticks, consumed eras in M1 "pess" mode.
Production unchanged.

## The rule barely filters anything
37% of all M1 bars touch the previous hour's high or low. Armed until the
end of the hour, 63% of bars are armed; on a 2 h rolling window, 99%.
Share of BOS signals let through:

| era | A/all | A/cont (primary) | B/all | B/cont | current 2 h rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R0 | 88% | 94% | 100% | 100% | 73% |
| 2022 | 86% | 92% | 100% | 100% | 70% |
| 2023 | 84% | 92% | 100% | 100% | 71% |
| 2024 | 86% | 93% | 99% | 100% | 72% |
| 2025 | 86% | 93% | 100% | 100% | 73% |
| 2026 H1 | 88% | 94% | 100% | 100% | 70% |

The 2 h variant (B) is indistinguishable from having no gate at all: in
every era its net is within a few dollars of NOGATE. It is not a rule.

It removes the wrong trades: the primary arm discards 22 trades worth
**+$67.88** versus NOGATE, while the current 2 h rule discards 143 trades
worth **−$80.04**. Continuations, which carry +$153.74 of the baseline's
+$236.83, collapse to −$35.17 under the touch gate.

**Random-mask control** (200 masks, same pass rate, M1 pess): mean
+$48.08 ± 33.06, 95th +$106.36; the primary arm scores **−$35.44, the
0.5th percentile**. 199 random ways of discarding the same share of
signals do better.

The primary arm loses less than BASELINE in 2023, 2025 and 2026 H1 and

trades each variant happened to drop, not skill. These eras cannot
validate a gate; they are reported for completeness.

## Pass criteria
Beats BASELINE in TRAIN: **no** (+0.07 vs +102.22). In TEST: **no**
(+18.57 vs +134.61). ≥ 95th percentile of random masks: **no** (0.5th).
Beats NOGATE per trade: **no** (+0.032 vs +0.258).

## Verdict: **C — not adopted.**

Touching the previous hour's extreme is close to a permanent state, so as
a gate it is either a no-op (2 h window) or, worse, a filter that removes
profitable continuations (hourly window). This is the E007 finding again:
the value of the current gate is not "weather", it is which trades get
skipped and how that frees the single position slot. Production
unchanged.
