# E011 — Independent validation of the frozen Owl strategy on pre-R0 Exness history

Proposed by ChatGPT, executed by Claude Code. Production untouched
throughout. Eras V2/V3/V4 were frozen in `DATA_ERAS.md` (commit 64c84c2)
before any download of the older years or any P&L. Order V2 → V3 → V4,
no change between eras, no rescue on any era. Inventory and the
July–September overlap validation are in `INVENTORY.md`.

Frozen strategy: candle-close FLIP-BOS + candle-close continuation, 2h
awake gate, pending-dot SL, TP 0.8R, one base position, fixed 0.02,
min_dist 10, tick execution (1 s poll) with the ACTUAL historical
bid/ask. Structure engine on M1 bars built from bid ticks (this
reproduces the R0 reference: +$238 vs +$237). Scripts: `e011_run_era.py`
(era report), `e011_control.py` (random-direction control),
`exness_import.py` (archive importer with SHA-256).

Reference: R0 (2026-07-01 → 09-08, MT5) +$237 on 524 trades, PF 1.28,
maxDD $83; V1 (09-08 → 09-11) −$26 on 47.

## Answers

1. **Does frozen Owl reproduce outside R0?** No. Every era loses; every half of every era loses.
2. **Stable across years?** Stably negative: PF 0.71–0.88, exp R −0.08 to −0.24 every year.
3. **Survives multiple BTC regimes?** No: bear 2022, recovery 2023, bull 2024, range 2025 and 2026 H1 all lose.
4. **Survives higher spreads?** No, and the $7 diagnostics show it would have lost in every era at today's cost as well (2022 −$552, 2023 −$297, 2024 −$279, 2025 −$713, 2026 H1 −$530). Spread explains roughly half of the historical loss, the signal the other half.
5. **Awake / stale-trend behaviour reproduces?** No. The gate contributes −$486 (2025), −$191 (2026 H1) and ±$100 of noise in 2022–2024; young-trend continuations are not better than stale ones anywhere.
6. **Flip and continuation consistent?** Consistently both negative.
7. **Beats random direction?** No: 6% (V2), 42% (V3), 21 / 13 / 62% (V4). E014 showed the signal direction is mildly contrarian at the event level.
8. **Was R0 representative?** No. R0 was one favourable 69-day stretch at the lowest spread in the instrument's history, selected by the research itself; its in-sample edge came from the interaction of gate and one-position selection (E007) rather than from the signal (E014).
9. **Confidence in deploying or growing Owl?** Decreases decisively.
10. **Is the current live size justified?** The evidence says the frozen strategy has negative expectancy outside its discovery window. Whether to keep it trading at 0.02 is the owner's decision; nothing in E011 supports adding size, and the −$60 kill line is the only protection in place. Production was not changed by this experiment.

## Verdict: **C — independent validation failure.**

The historical edge does not reproduce on any of the 3.5 years of independent Exness history. No tuning on the validation eras; all of them are consumed. Any future variant must be developed on R0 or on a new mechanism (E015/E016) and validated on data not yet seen.
