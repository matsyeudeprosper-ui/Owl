# E027 — first BOS of a flip only, stop at the true lowest low (2026-09-15)

Owner's idea, preregistered before results (`PREREG_E027.md`, 9f5f303):
take only the first BOS after a flip, and put the stop at the lowest low
of every candle in the leg rather than at the confirmed dot. The two
changes were run separately so the effect of each is visible.
Script `e027.py`. Production unchanged.

## The deep stop is almost the same stop
Measured on every signal of every era: the true lowest low of the leg
differs from the dot in **46–50%** of signals, but the **median
difference is 0 points** and the 90th percentile is 20 points. The
silence filter drops a candle on its *close*, and a candle that wicks
below the reference nearly always closes below it too, so it is kept and
its low already sets the dot. The stop being asked for is, in practice,
the stop the bot already uses.

Dropping the continuations is what costs: +$237 → +$50. The
continuations carry +$154 of the baseline's +$237, so the flip entry on
its own is near break-even. The deep stop then removes a further $15.
The random-direction control beats the primary arm in the TEST half.

Primary beats BASELINE in **1 of 5** eras. The deep stop is worse than
the dot stop in **all five** eras, on both entry sets — small but
perfectly consistent, which fits the measurement above: it moves the stop
a few points further away for no information gain, and pays for it. The
random-direction control beats the primary in 3 of 5 eras.

## Pass criteria
Beats BASELINE on expectancy in TRAIN and TEST: **no** (+0.160 vs +0.395,
+0.048 vs +0.508). In ≥ 3 of 5 eras: **no** (1/5). Beats its own
random-direction control: **no** (loses in the TEST half and in 3 eras).

## Verdict: **C — not adopted.**

Two clean lessons rather than one. Restricting to the first BOS removes
the profitable half of the strategy: the continuations, not the flips,
are what pay on R0. And the "deeper" stop the idea asks for is already
the stop in use half the time and within 20 points of it the rest of the
time, so there is nothing there to gain; where it differs it costs a
little.

Production unchanged.
