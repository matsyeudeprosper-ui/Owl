## Why

- The touch rule's backtest profit depended on tick-level execution that
  the M1 backtest could not see: about a third of its paper profit
  disappears on real ticks, and on its own the touch entry is breakeven
  (+$8 on 493 trades).
- The candle-close rule has no execution bias and loses only $8 to
  observed slippage where touch loses $45.
- Neither rule is confirmed by the untouched 3 days. This change makes
  the bot more honest and more robust, not proven profitable. The
  100-trade review and the −$60 kill line stay as they were.
